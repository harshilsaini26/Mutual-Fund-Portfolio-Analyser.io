    Your data stays yours

    Nothing you enter leaves your browser

    Your portfolio and your settings are kept in this browser's own storage, on this device. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere or kept on any server. You can clear them at any time: Clear on Your portfolio, Reset to defaults in Settings.

    How this works
    • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics.
    • The page loads nothing from any other site: its content security policy allows only this one.
    • Like any web host, Vercel keeps ordinary request logs (the address and the page asked for), never what you type into a page.
