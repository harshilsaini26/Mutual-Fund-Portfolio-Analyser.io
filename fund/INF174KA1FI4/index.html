    3 notes about this data
    • AMFI's older heading, which its newer one splits into sectoral and thematic funds; which of the two each fund here became is not recorded, so they are compared only with each other.
    • Rank 1 is the highest return, the lowest volatility, the smallest fall, the highest return for the risk and the lowest expense ratio. Each counts only the category's funds with that figure (a year of prices for one-year figures, three or five years for those, an expense ratio on record for the cost), so the totals differ.
    • Ranks compare the funds open today; funds that closed or merged are not included, which tends to flatter the category's middle.
