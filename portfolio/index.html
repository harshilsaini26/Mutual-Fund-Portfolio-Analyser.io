    Your portfolio

    What your funds are worth, and what they own

    Pick the funds you hold and enter what you put in, as lump sums or SIPs. The figures are worked out in this browser from the prices and holdings this site publishes; nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

    Direct plans are listed. A Regular plan can be entered as its Direct version; its value here then reads higher than your statement, by the difference in cost.

    XIRRA yearly return for money that went in on different dates and in different amounts, such as a SIP. Each amount is counted from its own date. For one purchase held more than a year it equals the annualised return; over less than a year it can look extreme, because a short gain is scaled up to a year.More in the glossary · Zerodha VarsityPortfolio overlapHow much of two funds' portfolios is the same companies. For each company both hold, the smaller of its two weights is counted, and the counts are added: 100% means identical portfolios, 0% nothing in common. Funds with a high overlap tend to rise and fall together.More in the glossary · Zerodha Varsity
